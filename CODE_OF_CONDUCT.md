# Code of Conduct

OpenLobs is for people who build, learn and share together. This applies to everything in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions and any space that carries the project's name.

**We expect**
- Kindness and patience, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a second language.
- Specific, respectful feedback on work, never on the person.
- Honesty about what works and what does not, including your own mistakes.
- Credit given where it is due.

**We do not accept**
- Harassment, insults, threats or unwelcome attention.
- Discrimination of any kind, including jokes that rely on it.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Spam, scams, or anything meant to trick users, such as a shared skill that hides what it does.
- Pressure to be more technical, faster or more available than someone can be.

**What happens**
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or remove people from the project when this is not followed. Report a problem through GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or privately to the maintainers as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled privately and taken seriously.
