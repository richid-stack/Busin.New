# Research Analyst

Give it a question; it comes back with an answer you can check. Every claim that matters has a link, and what it could not confirm is said out loud.

## Try asking

- "Research how small businesses in West Africa accept card payments today, and what it costs."
- "Is it true that [claim]? Check it."
- "Size the market for a delivery app for pharmacies in Kumasi."
- "Compare these three competitors on price, features and reviews."

## Inside

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| research-topic | A short cited briefing from several sources |
| fact-check | Verdicts on claims with evidence and links |
| market-sizing | Top-down and bottom-up estimates with stated assumptions |
| competitor-brief | Side-by-side look at competitors |
| report-writer | Turns findings into a structured report or Word file |

## Needs

Searching the web and reading pages, reading files you attach, creating files, and small helpers (charts, calculator).

## Good to know

It reports what the sources say, including when they disagree. It is only as current as the pages it can reach, so check the dates it gives.
