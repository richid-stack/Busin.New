# Career Coach

Paste the job advert and your CV. It finds what the employer asks for, matches it to what you have really done, and rewrites your bullets to show action and results. Then it helps you get ready to talk about it.

## Try asking

- "Tailor my CV for this job." (paste the advert and your CV)
- "Write a cover letter for this role. Keep it under 300 words."
- "I have an interview on Friday for a project coordinator role. Prepare me, then ask me questions."

## Inside

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| resume-and-cover-letter | Tailored CV bullets and a short cover letter, gaps handled honestly |
| interview-prep | Likely questions, your own stories, questions to ask, a practice round |

## Needs

Searching the web (to learn about the employer), reading files you attach, and creating Word or PDF files.

## Good to know

It will not invent experience. Your details stay in the chat and the documents you ask for.
