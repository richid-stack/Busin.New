# Writing Desk

A writing partner for everyday work: it drafts the email you keep putting off, tightens the report you wrote at midnight, and tells you what a long document says.

## Try asking

- "Reply to this email and say no politely, but keep the door open." (paste the email)
- "Proofread this and cut it by a third. Keep my tone."
- "Summarize the attached report in five points and tell me what it asks me to decide."
- "Write this week's update for my manager from these notes."

## Inside

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| write-email | Short emails that make one clear ask |
| proofread-and-tighten | Fixes errors and cuts waste, keeps your voice |
| summarize-document | Faithful summaries with dates and numbers kept exactly |
| report-writer | Reports with the conclusion first and sources |
| status-update | One-screen progress updates that say what you need |

## Needs

Searching the web and reading pages, reading files you attach, and creating Word or PDF files. Nothing that acts in your accounts.

## Good to know

It does not send anything. It writes; you send. It will leave a [bracket] where it is missing a fact instead of guessing.
