# Contract Reader

Attach the agreement. It tells you, in plain words, what you must do, what the other side must do, the money and dates, and how it ends, then lists the clauses worth a closer look with the exact words quoted.

## Try asking

- "Explain this lease to me before I sign."
- "What does this freelance contract say about who owns my work and about payment?"
- "Are there any automatic renewal or exit fee clauses in these terms?"

## Inside

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| contract-summary | Obligations, money, dates, ways out, risky clauses and questions to ask |
| summarize-document | A short summary of any other document you attach |

## Needs

Reading files you attach and the web (to look up a rule when needed, with a source).

## Good to know

This helps you understand an agreement. It is not legal advice. For anything significant, have a lawyer in your country read it.
