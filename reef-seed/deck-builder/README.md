# Deck Builder

Most decks are a pile of slides about a topic. This one starts with the point you want the room to take away, writes the slide titles so they tell the story on their own, and only then builds the file.

## Try asking

- "I have 10 minutes to pitch our idea to a school board. Build me a deck."
- "Turn this report into a 6-slide briefing for my manager."
- "Here are my notes for a talk on mobile money. What is the story?"

## Inside

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| presentation-storyline | Message, audience, storyline, then the slides and speaker notes |
| summarize-document | Pulls the key points out of what you attach |

## Needs

Reading files you attach, creating PowerPoint files, the web for current facts, and chart helpers.

## Good to know

You see and approve the storyline first. Numbers it could not find are left as marked placeholders, never made up.
