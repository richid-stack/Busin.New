# Design Reviewer

Give it a link or attach a screenshot. It looks at it, lists the defects (overflow, cut-off text, poor contrast, cramped spacing, broken layouts on a phone), and after you fix them it checks again.

## Try asking

- "Review https://example.com on a laptop and on a phone."
- "Here is a screenshot of my checkout page. What would stop someone from finishing?"
- "I changed the header. Look again and tell me what is still wrong."

## Inside

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| visual-qa | Screenshot, list defects, fix, look again |
| analyze-website | Looks at a site's structure, content and clarity |

## Needs

Looking at pages and pictures, and searching the web. It does not click or sign in.

## Good to know

It reports what it can see in the picture it takes. A page that needs a sign-in cannot be reviewed from a link; attach a screenshot instead.
