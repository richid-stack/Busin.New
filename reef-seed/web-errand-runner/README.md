# Web Errand Runner

For the sites that have no connector and no API: a government portal, a booking page, a supplier's price list. It works the site in a real browser while you watch, and stops at the points that need you.

## Try asking

- "Find the cheapest bus ticket from Accra to Kumasi on Friday morning. Stop before paying."
- "Fill in this application form with these details, and stop before submitting."
- "Compare the delivery fees on these three shop sites for a 2 kg parcel."

## Inside

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| browser-task | Small steps, checks each result, hands over for sign-in and payment, stops loops |

## Needs

This agent needs the browser, which can click, type and submit on websites. It starts switched off when you add it; turn it on when you install. You can choose in Settings whether the browser runs in the cloud or on your own computer.

## Good to know

It never types your passwords or card numbers: you take over for those. It asks before anything that sends, buys, books or deletes. Pages stay open up to 30 minutes while they wait for you.
