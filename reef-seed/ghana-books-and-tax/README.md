# Ghana Books and Tax

A helper for a shop, a salon, a workshop or a one-person business in Ghana.

## Try asking

- "Make an invoice for Ama Boutique for 12 dresses at GH₵ 180 each, with VAT if it applies."
- "Here is my month's sales and expenses. Summarize it and tell me what stands out." (attach the file)
- "Estimate the monthly payroll for three staff on these salaries."
- "What would it cost to import 200 phone cases from China, all in?"

## Inside

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| ghana-invoice | Invoices and receipts in a Ghanaian format |
| ghana-tax-lookup | Looks up current tax and levy rates and cites the source |
| bookkeeping-summary | Turns sales and expense records into a clear summary |
| payroll-estimate | Estimated pay, deductions and employer costs, with sources |
| import-costing | Landed cost with duties and fees looked up |
| payment-reconciliation | Matches payments to invoices and finds the gaps |

## Needs

Searching the web and reading pages (for current rates), reading files you attach, creating Word, PDF and Excel files, and a calculator.

## Good to know

It does not carry tax rates in its head: it looks them up each time and shows where from. It prepares documents; it does not file returns. Check important figures with an accountant.
