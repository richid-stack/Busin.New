# Project Manager

For the person who has been handed "make this happen" with a date and a vague brief.

## Try asking

- "Plan a product launch in 6 weeks. We are three people. Make a tracker."
- "Here are my 14 tasks. What do I do first, and what do I drop?"
- "Turn these meeting notes into decisions and action items with owners."
- "We missed the deadline. Help me write a postmortem without blaming anyone."

## Inside

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| project-plan | Scope, milestones, owners, risks and a tracker spreadsheet |
| prioritize-work | Do now, schedule, delegate, drop, with reasons |
| status-update | One-screen updates with the asks made explicit |
| meeting-notes | Decisions, actions, owners and open questions |
| postmortem | Timeline, causes and actions, no blame |

## Needs

Reading files you attach, creating files and spreadsheets, and small helpers (plan, calculator).

## Good to know

It plans from what you tell it. It does not know your team's real availability, so it asks.
