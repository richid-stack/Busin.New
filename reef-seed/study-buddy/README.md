# Study Buddy

Attach your notes or a chapter, or just name the topic. It explains the idea, gives you worked examples, writes practice questions with answers kept separate, and helps you plan the time you have.

## Try asking

- "Explain compound interest to me like I have never seen it, then give me practice problems."
- "Make a study guide from these notes for my exam on Friday."
- "Quiz me on photosynthesis, one question at a time, and go harder when I get it right."

## Inside

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| study-guide | Explanations, key terms, worked examples, practice questions, a study plan |

## Needs

Searching the web (for facts that change or exam details), reading files you attach, and small helpers (charts, calculator).

## Good to know

It helps you learn the method rather than handing over answers to coursework. If it is not sure, it says so and shows how to check.
