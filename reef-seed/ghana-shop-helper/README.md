# Ghana Shop Helper

For the owner who answers customers on WhatsApp between serving the next person.

## Try asking

- "A customer says the dress she got is the wrong size and wants her money back. Write a reply."
- "Ama owes me GH₵ 1,200 for three weeks. Write a polite reminder for WhatsApp."
- "I buy bags at GH₵ 45 and sell at GH₵ 70. What is my margin, and what should I charge to make 40%?"
- "Here is my week. What should I look at on Monday?"

## Inside

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| customer-reply | Warm, clear replies to questions, complaints and requests |
| payment-reminder | Reminders from a friendly nudge to a firm final notice, never a threat |
| pricing-and-margins | Cost, margin and price, with the working shown |
| weekly-business-review | A short look back and what to do next |
| cash-flow-forecast | What is coming in and going out over the next weeks |

## Needs

Searching the web, creating files and spreadsheets, and a calculator. It does not reach your WhatsApp, bank or accounts.

## Good to know

It writes; you send. It asks for what it is missing instead of guessing amounts.
