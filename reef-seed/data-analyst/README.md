# Data Analyst

Attach a spreadsheet or CSV and ask what it shows. It checks the data first, answers your question with the numbers visible, and draws a chart that carries the main finding.

## Try asking

- "What stands out in this sales file? Which month and which product moved most?"
- "Check this budget: do the totals add up?"
- "Build me a monthly cash budget for a shop with these inputs, with a chart and checks."
- "Clean up these customer names and dates, and tell me what you changed."

## Inside

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| analyze-data | Cleans, checks, finds patterns, reports with a chart |
| build-spreadsheet-model | Workbooks with an inputs sheet, live formulas and checks |

## Needs

Reading files you attach, creating spreadsheets, charts and a calculator, and the web for rates and prices.

## Good to know

It never overwrites your file; it saves a new one. It reports data problems instead of quietly dropping rows. Very large files may be read in part, and it says so.
