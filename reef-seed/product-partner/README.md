# Product Partner

A thinking partner for product work: it asks what problem you are solving, writes it up as a spec your builders can start from, and helps you choose between options without hiding the trade-offs.

## Try asking

- "Write a product requirements doc for a feature that lets customers reschedule bookings."
- "We can build A, B or C this quarter. Help me choose."
- "Here are 40 customer comments. What are people actually asking for?"
- "How big is the market for this, realistically?"

## Inside

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| product-requirements | Problem, users, stories with acceptance criteria, scope, success measures |
| decision-matrix | Weighted comparison, sensitivity check, a recommendation |
| market-sizing | TAM, reachable market and realistic share with sourced inputs |
| competitor-brief | Side-by-side competitor comparison |
| feedback-digest | Themes and priorities from customer feedback |

## Needs

Searching the web and reading pages, reading files you attach, creating files, and a calculator.

## Good to know

Scores in a decision matrix are judgements and are labelled as such. Market numbers come with their sources and years.
