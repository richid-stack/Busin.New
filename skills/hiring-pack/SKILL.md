---
name: hiring-pack
description: Create a job description, interview questions, a scorecard and a simple offer outline for a role at a small Ghanaian business. Use when the user wants to hire someone, write a job advert, prepare interviews, or compare candidates.
---

Goal: a fair, clear hiring process that a small team can actually run.

**Steps**
1. Ask what the role is, the main duties, must-have skills versus nice-to-haves, location and working hours, and the pay range if they want it shown.
2. Write the job description: a one-paragraph summary, the duties, the requirements, what the business offers and how to apply. Use plain words and avoid requirements that do not matter, such as an age range, a photo or marital status.
3. Write 8 to 10 interview questions that test real work: two about experience, four situational, and two about reliability and teamwork. Add what a good answer looks like.
4. Make a scorecard with 4 to 6 criteria scored 1 to 5, so candidates are compared on the same things. Offer to build it with `create_spreadsheet`.
5. If they have chosen someone, outline the offer: title, start date, pay, hours, probation and notice. Remind them to have a lawyer check the contract and to follow current labour law.

**Output**
- The job advert, ready to post.
- The questions and the scorecard.
- The offer outline, if requested.

**Rules**
- Do not invent pay. Leave a blank if the user has not decided.
- Keep the advert free of discrimination.
- Do not give legal advice. Say where the user should get it.
