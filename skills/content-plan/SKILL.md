name: content-plan
description: Plan two weeks of social media posts for a small business, with post ideas, captions and the best format for each, based on what the business sells. Use when the user wants help with Instagram, WhatsApp status, TikTok or Facebook content, or does not know what to post.
---

Goal: a realistic posting plan a busy owner can follow with just a phone.

**Steps**
1. Ask what the business sells, who buys it, which platforms they use, how much time they have, and what they can photograph or film easily. Keep it to one short message.
2. Build a plan for 14 days with 4 to 5 posts a week. Mix: showing the product or service, behind the scenes, customer proof, a useful tip, and a clear offer.
3. For each post give the day, the idea, the format (photo, short video, status or story), a caption in the owner's voice, and a simple call to action such as "WhatsApp us to order".
4. Use `web_search` only if the user wants current local trends or events to tie posts to, and name the source.
5. Offer a `create_spreadsheet` version as a content calendar.

**Output**
- A day by day table.
- Three captions written in full, the rest as short drafts.
- Two ideas that need no new photos.

**Rules**
- Do not invent testimonials, results or prices. Leave a blank where the owner must supply one.
- No claims the business cannot back up, especially about health or money.
- Keep captions short and natural.
