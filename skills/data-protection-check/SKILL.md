---
name: data-protection-check
description: Check how a small business handles the personal data of its customers against Ghana's data protection rules and produce a plain checklist and a privacy notice draft. Use when the user collects customer details, runs an app or website, asks about the Data Protection Act, registering with the Data Protection Commission, or needs a privacy policy.
---

Goal: a practical list of what to fix, based on the law as it is now, written for a non-lawyer.

**Steps**
1. Ask what personal data the business collects (names, phone numbers, addresses, ID numbers, photos, payment details), why, where it is stored, who else sees it, and how long it is kept.
2. Look up the current rules with `web_search` and `web_fetch`, preferring the Data Protection Commission and the text of the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and any newer guidance. Check what registration is required and how to renew it.
3. Compare what the business does to the principles you found: a clear purpose, only the data needed, consent where needed, safe storage, a way for people to see or correct their data, and deletion when it is no longer needed.
4. Write a checklist of gaps, ordered by risk, each with one specific fix.
5. Draft a short plain-language privacy notice using only the facts the user gave. Leave blanks for contact details.

**Output**
- **What to do first**, three items.
- The checklist with status: fine, fix, or unclear.
- The privacy notice draft.
- **Questions for a lawyer**, where the rules depend on the facts.

**Rules**
- Cite the source for every legal point and say when it was read.
- Do not say a business is compliant. Say what you checked and what remains.
- This is guidance, not legal advice.
