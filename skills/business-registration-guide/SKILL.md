---
name: business-registration-guide
description: Explain the steps, offices and documents needed to register or formalise a business in Ghana, based on current official sources. Use when the user asks how to register a company or business name, get a TIN, register for VAT, SSNIT or other licences, or what they need to operate legally.
---

Goal: a practical, ordered checklist for this person's situation, from sources that are current.

**Steps**
1. Ask what you need in one message: sole proprietor or company, what the business does (some activities need extra licences), where it operates, and whether they will employ people.
2. Research with `web_search` and `web_fetch`, preferring official sites: the Office of the Registrar of Companies, the Ghana Revenue Authority, SSNIT, the Data Protection Commission, the Food and Drugs Authority and the local assembly where relevant. Note fees and processing times only if you read them on an official page, with the date.
3. Order the steps in the sequence a person would do them, and say which can happen in parallel.
4. Point out the common sticking points you find in sources, such as name approval, a Ghana Card for directors and a registered address.

**Output**
- **Your path**, a numbered list: what to do, where, what to bring.
- **Costs and timing** only where a source states them, with the source.
- **Later obligations**: filings and renewals the business will face after registering.
- **Questions to ask a lawyer or accountant** where the answer depends on the details.

**Rules**
- Do not quote a fee, form number or website you did not read in this conversation.
- If the official site is unreadable, say so and give the general order of steps clearly marked as unconfirmed.
- This is guidance, not legal advice.
